# Tatros Design Principles

## Our Philosophy

Simple.
Clear.
Purposeful.

If something doesn't help the user, remove it.

---

# Rule 1

One message per section.

---

# Rule 2

One primary action per screen.

---

# Rule 3

Maximum three sentences per paragraph.

---

# Rule 4

Use plain English.

Avoid technical jargon.

---

# Rule 5

Show more.

Explain less.

---

# Rule 6

Whitespace is a feature.

Never fill space just because it exists.

---

# Rule 7

Every section answers one question.

Hero
Who are we?

Services
What do we do?

Why Tatros
Why trust us?

Contact
How do we get started?

---

# Rule 8

If a sentence can be shorter,
make it shorter.

---

# Rule 9

Every page must have one clear CTA.

---

# Rule 10

Design for clarity before creativity.
