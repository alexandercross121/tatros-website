Delivery Process

A controlled path from opportunity to operation

Each engagement adapts to its context while retaining explicit decisions, evidence, quality controls, and ownership.

  1. Discover

    Establish outcomes, users, constraints, risks, and decision criteria.

  2. Define

    Prioritize scope, architecture, governance, measures, and delivery plan.

  3. Deliver

    Design and build in reviewable increments with transparent progress.

  4. Validate

    Test usability, accessibility, security, performance, and readiness.

  5. Operate

    Launch, monitor outcomes, transfer knowledge, and improve responsibly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning an engagement

Answers to common questions about scope, delivery, and collaboration.

Can services be combined into one engagement?

Yes. Tatros can combine advisory, design, engineering, cloud, AI, and operational support around one outcome with clear ownership across workstreams.

How is the final scope determined?

Scope follows a structured discussion of objectives, users, current systems, dependencies, risks, budget context, timing, and evidence required for success.

Can Tatros work with our internal team or existing vendors?

Yes. Responsibilities, interfaces, decision rights, and knowledge-transfer expectations are defined early so internal teams and partners can work effectively together.

Are the timelines on this page guaranteed?

No. They are planning ranges. A delivery estimate is produced after dependencies, assurance requirements, procurement, content, integrations, and scope are understood.

How are accessibility and security handled?

Both are lifecycle requirements. Applicable standards, threats, controls, testing, evidence, and ongoing ownership are defined for the specific engagement.

Define the right next step for your technology initiative

Share the outcome you need, the current environment, and the constraints that matter. Tatros will help frame a practical engagement path.
