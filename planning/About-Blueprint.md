# About Page Blueprint

## Objective

Build trust by explaining who Tatros is, what the company believes, and how it
helps organizations succeed through technology.

The About page should complement the Homepage, not repeat it.

---

## Audience

- Business Owners
- Government Organizations
- Startups
- SMEs
- Enterprise Clients
- Decision Makers

---

## User Questions

Visitors should leave this page knowing:

- Who is Tatros?
- Why was Tatros created?
- What makes Tatros different?
- How does Tatros work?
- Why should I trust Tatros?
- What should I do next?

---

## Success Criteria

A visitor should understand Tatros in less than 60 seconds.

The page should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Honest
- Human
- Confident

---

## Design Principles

- One message per section
- Short headings
- Short paragraphs
- Plenty of white space
- Accessible
- Mobile-first
- Reuse existing components
