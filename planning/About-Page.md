# About Page

## Objective

Help visitors understand who Tatros is, what the company believes, and how it delivers technology solutions.

The About page should build trust without repeating the homepage.

---

## Audience

- Business owners
- Government organizations
- SMEs
- Enterprise clients
- Decision makers

---

## Success Criteria

Visitors should understand:

- Who Tatros is
- What Tatros does
- Why Tatros exists
- How Tatros works
- Why they should trust Tatros
- What to do next

---

## Design Principles

- One message per section
- Short headings
- Short paragraphs
- Plenty of white space
- No marketing fluff
- No fake statistics
- No fake testimonials
